# 2026-09-09

- **Web acquisition became a first-class layer.** Research sessions kept hitting WAF
  blocks, JS-only pages and robots directives naming AI agents, and the graph had nothing
  to say about any of it — the entire web surface was one roster row in
  [[ingestion-toolchain]] deferring to "a full browser fetch" that was defined nowhere.
  Distilled into [[web-acquisition-ladder]] (seven rungs, T0–T6) with
  [[2026-09-09-web-acquisition-research]] as its capture. Linked from [[moc-meta]];
  [[research-flow]] step 2 and [[search-gates]] now point at it for the fetch budget,
  which is a different scale from their stakes tiers.

- **The measured probes changed the design more than the reading did.** Swapping in a full
  Chrome User-Agent changed nothing (reuters 401, g2 403, identical both ways) — the block
  is at the TLS fingerprint layer. Meanwhile stdlib `urllib` with an honest UA got 200 from
  NYT at 1.28 MB despite its robots.txt disallowing `anthropic-ai`, and Wayback returned a
  59 KB 2026 snapshot of a Reuters page that answers 401 live. So the ladder puts archives
  and official APIs *above* stealth, and rates fingerprint impersonation amber-red rather
  than treating it as the obvious next step.

- **An interrupted research run was recovered rather than re-run.** 21 subagents produced
  reports; a quota cut delivered only 7, and the structured-access coordinator returned a
  40-character stub — which is exactly why the first draft's tier-0 roster and own-jurisdiction
  legal section were thin. The 13 orphaned reports (509 KB) were recovered from disk and
  drained. Four corrections came out of that and are recorded in the capture: *Amazon v.
  Perplexity* had been stated far too broadly (it is a PI appeal, CFAA/CDAFA "access" only,
  with footnote 5 preserving ToS regulation and the holding conditional on the fetch
  originating from the user's own browser); the other three concerned the author's own
  jurisdiction and live with that analysis in the private zone, not in this template.

- **`raw/` drained.** The 13 recovered reports went to `.personal-shared/`, not to tracked
  `archive/`: one of them records this machine's egress IP, and the template repo is public.
  The zone's contract — material the graph reasons from that git must not hold — is the
  exact fit, and `.personal-shared/README.md` now indexes them. `raw/` is back to its
  README alone.

- **Environment facts recorded, with provenance per line.** `.personal-shared/README.md`
  had none, so every prior session had been probing a shell and reporting it as the user's
  machine. Two live gaps fell out of doing it: `pandoc` is named in the ingestion roster but
  is not installed, and there is no JavaScript runtime, which silently degrades `yt-dlp`'s
  YouTube path. Both are now written down where the roster can be checked against reality.

- **`yt-transcript` now prefers `json3`.** YouTube's native timed-text JSON has no rolling
  duplicates, so the recipe's original reason for existing — collapsing repeated VTT cues —
  becomes a fallback path rather than the main road. VTT handling stays for everything else.
